Privacy Policy — Hazir Ji

Last updated: August 2026

Hazir Ji ("the App") is an offline employee attendance and salary management app for small shops. This page explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

1. Data Stored Locally On Your Device

Employee records, attendance, salary, overtime, advances, and reports are stored locally on your own device (offline). This data is not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

2. Account Information

To use the App, you create an account with a mobile number and password, or sign in with your Pi Network account. This account information is stored securely with our backend provider (Supabase) solely to authenticate you and manage access approval.

3. Pi Network Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Pi Network, we receive your Pi username and a verification token from Pi Network to confirm your identity. We do not receive your Pi wallet passphrase or private keys.

4. Pi Payments

If you make a payment through the App using Pi Network, the payment is processed directly through Pi Network's official payment system. We receive only the payment confirmation needed to verify your transaction.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except as required to operate the App (such as our backend hosting provider) or as required by law.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the app developer.
