Hazir Ji – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Hazir Ji is an attendance and salary management application designed for small shops and businesses.

2. Information Used

The app allows users to enter employee names, attendance records, salary details, overtime, late hours, advances and payment balances.

3. Data Storage

Hazir Ji is designed primarily for offline use. Employee and salary records are intended to remain on the user's device.

4. Pi Network

If users access Pi Network sign-in, authentication may be handled by Pi Network services under their applicable privacy policies.

5. Data Sharing

Hazir Ji does not intend to sell employee or salary records to third parties.

6. Security

Users are responsible for securing their devices and protecting access to their records.

7. Contact

Developer: Kulwant Bangarh Mansa

App: Hazir Ji

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact details provided in the app listing.
